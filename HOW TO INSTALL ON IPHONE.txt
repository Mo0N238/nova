NOVA FOR iPHONE - HOW TO INSTALL
================================

Nova needs a home on the internet (a free web address) so your iPhone can
install her. GitHub Pages hosts her for free. You can do all of this on
your iPhone in about 10 minutes.

STEP 1 - Unzip Nova
  Tap Nova-iPhone.zip in the Files app. It turns into a folder called
  "Nova-iPhone" with 7 files inside.

STEP 2 - Make a free GitHub account
  Go to github.com in Safari and sign up (free). Remember your username.

STEP 3 - Make a home for Nova
  1. Tap the + at the top, then "New repository".
  2. Name it:  nova
  3. Choose Public, and tick "Add a README file".
  4. Tap "Create repository".

STEP 4 - Upload Nova
  1. In your new "nova" repository, tap "Add file", then "Upload files".
  2. Tap "choose your files", open the Nova-iPhone folder, select ALL the
     files inside it (index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, the 3 icons,
     and this guide), and upload them.
     Upload the files themselves, not the folder.
  3. Tap "Commit changes".

STEP 5 - Turn on the web address
  1. In the repository, open Settings, then "Pages" (in the menu).
  2. Under "Branch", pick "main" and "/ (root)", then tap Save.
  3. Wait 1-2 minutes. Your address will be:
        https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/nova/

STEP 6 - Put Nova on your home screen
  1. Open that address in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then "Add to Home Screen",
     then "Add".
  3. Open Nova from her new icon. From now on, always open her from the icon.

STEP 7 - Give Nova her brain (one time)
  1. Nova shows a setup screen. Tap the aistudio.google.com/apikey link.
  2. Sign in with Google, tap "Create API key", and copy it.
  3. Go back to Nova, paste the key, add your name, and tap "Wake Nova up".

That's it. Nova is her own app now. She doesn't need Claude or your PC.


GOOD TO KNOW
  - Your key, tasks and chats are stored only on your phone. The GitHub page
    is public, but it only holds Nova's app files, never your key or data.
  - Reminders pop up while Nova is open. Tap the calendar button on any task
    to add it to your iPhone Calendar, so you get a real alert every day even
    when she's closed.
  - Voice: in the You tab, turn on "Speak replies out loud". Tap the
    microphone (or the glowing orb) to talk to her.
  - The free Google brain has a daily limit. If she says she's hit it, she's
    back after midnight Pacific time. Your tasks still work.
  - Back up your stuff now and then: You tab, then "Back up".
  - Deleting Nova from your home screen deletes her data too, so back up
    first.

UPDATING NOVA LATER
  Upload the new index.html to the same GitHub repository (Add file, then
  Upload files, then Commit). Close and reopen Nova twice to pick it up.
