What Nova does

Everything lives in one app on your home screen. Tap her, talk, and get on with your day.

Talk or type

Tap the mic, say it, pause: she sends it herself. Quick math is instant, and one-right-answer questions get the answer first.

Your day, handled

Tasks, your schedule, reminders and timers, a week view that catches double-bookings, and notifications even when Nova is closed.

She remembers

Tell her something once and she keeps it in mind, so the next answer actually fits your life.

Knows when to be serious

Playful by default. Say "no seriously" or "for real" and the jokes stop until you laugh again.

Connect with friends

Add friends by username or friend code. Post, chat, like, and see who's top of the game leaderboards.

Three games built in

Starhop, the main event. Plus Nova Run, a neon runner with its own lo-fi soundtrack, and Nova Blocks, a falling-blocks puzzle.

Nova's main game

Starhop

A bright cartoon platformer that feels nothing like Nova. Pick a hero, hop through 3 worlds and 9 levels, grab stars, find the 3 big stars in every level, and bop the grumpy locals.

Meadow Hills, Sugar Peaks, Cloud Kingdom. On-screen buttons or swipe controls, chiptune music, and your total goes on the Connect leaderboard.
