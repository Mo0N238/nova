One-time setup: give Nova her brain.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key (free, no card needed).
  3. Copy the key and paste it here.

Already have a Groq key (starts with gsk_)? That works too: paste it instead.

Your key is saved only on this phone. Nova talks straight to Google; there's no other server.
